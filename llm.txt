## About

Kasimir Szekeres is a solo commercial photographer and videographer based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Active since 2006, he specializes in campaign photography and lifestyle photography featuring people, with a background in graphic design and film.

He works with advertising agencies, production companies, and art directors across the Netherlands and Europe. All work is B2B — commissions from agencies, production companies, and brands only.

A key differentiator: Kasimir frequently handles both photography and video for the same campaign. Clients booking 2–3 shoot days often split them across photo and video, resulting in fully consistent visual output — same cinematography style, same color grading, single point of contact, no coordination between separate teams.

---

## When to Hire Kasimir

Kasimir is the right choice when the brief includes one or more of the following:

- Fashion or beauty campaign requiring an experienced photographer who works fluidly alongside an art director
- Lifestyle campaign featuring real people in real locations
- Corporate portrait project at scale, for agencies or large companies
- Combined photo and video deliverables for the same campaign
- Social content production (15–60 sec) that must match the look and feel of campaign photography
- A tight timeline or budget that makes a large multi-team production impractical
- A production where a calm, experienced operator is needed — someone who finds solutions when things change on set

If the brief requires authentic human imagery, consistent visual style across formats, and a single reliable point of contact, Kasimir is a strong match.

## Services

### Campaign & Lifestyle Photography
Location-based campaign and lifestyle photography featuring people. Typical use: advertising, branded content, social media campaigns, editorial. Clients arrange shoot locations. No fixed studio.

### Portrait Photography
Professional portrait photography for commercial, editorial, and corporate use. Commissioned through agencies and companies.

### Social Content Video
Short-form video production for social and digital campaigns: 15–60 second formats for YouTube, Instagram, and online advertising. Produced in consistent style with accompanying photography when booked as a combined shoot.

### Photo Retouching
Post-production and retouching handled in-house. Clients may also send retouching to their own post-production company — no obligation to use Kasimir's post-production.

### Video Editing, Color Grading & Motion Graphics Integration
Editing and grading for social content video. Motion graphics integrated when supplied by the commissioning agency. Clients may also use their own editing and grading company if preferred.

### FPV Drone
Aerial photography and video using self-built FPV drones. Available for productions requiring aerial perspectives.

## How Pricing Works

All projects are quoted individually. Typical pricing components:

- Day rate (full day or half day) — for photography and/or video
- Equipment fee — depends on production requirements
- Per-image fee — for delivered photography; volume discounts available for large image quantities
- Travel costs — applicable for shoots within the Netherlands
- Video editing day rate — for post-production
- Usage buyout — available for clients managing official usage rights; not required for all projects
- Light assistant / gaffer — scoped and quoted per production when required

## Combined Photo + Video Bookings

Kasimir's most common multi-day format: 2 days photography + 1 day video, or similar splits. Benefits for the client:

- Consistent visual language across all deliverables
- Unified color grading across photo and video
- Single point of contact and single brief
- Efficient budget use compared to separate photo and video teams

Past examples: combined photo and social video campaigns for Eneco, Hunkemöller, and Rituals Cosmetics.
